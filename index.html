I.

About

Kristoff recently completed an MA in the Master of Arts Program in the Social Sciences at the University of Chicago, concentrating in Quantitative Methods and Social Analysis. His work sits at the intersection of comparative authoritarianism, quantitative methods, and Chinese politics, with particular interests in transnational repression, emergency and disaster governance, and formal models of bureaucratic behavior under dictatorship.

He is currently a student fellow at the Chicago Center on Democracy, directed by Prof. Susan Stokes (Chair, American Political Science Association), and a research assistant on the Civic Participation in China Survey (CPCS), a five-wave online survey of urban Chinese residents run by a five-institution team, under the supervision of Prof. Jessica C. Teets (Middlebury College). At UChicago, he supports Prof. Dali Yang’s research on political attitudes before and after the COVID-19 pandemic using Chinese General Social Survey (CGSS) data.

Research

Working Paper
Mar 2026 –
Present

Grassroots Infrastructural Power and Local Policy Responsiveness under Campaign-Style Governance in China

University of Chicago

Develops a theory of variation in campaign-style governance across subnational units in China; argues that grassroots infrastructural power conditions the intensity of local policy implementation. Constructs a novel subnational panel dataset combining neighborhood-committee data with OxCGRT policy indicators, and empirically tests variation in Zero-COVID policy responses (2020–2022) via hierarchical mixed-effects models.

Publication
Spring 2026

Cao, Kristoff. “Domestic Politics and Transnational Repression: Evidence from a Zero-Inflated Poisson Model.”

Undergraduate Journal of Humanistic Studies (Carleton College), vol. 19 — carleton.edu/ujhs

Replicated a large-N analysis of domestic determinants of transnational repression and extended it methodologically by fitting a zero-inflated Poisson model, uncovering significant results and pathways for future theory-building.
